One vendor instead of many

Eddnark Company Limited was registered in August 2017 as a service provider, set up to deliver quality solutions to specification. We are in the heart of Takoradi, in Ghana's Western Region.

Clients often lose time sourcing equipment from several vendors, and dealing with providers who fall short on reliability and quality. We keep a wide inventory under one roof, so you negotiate once and receive dependable support throughout the job.

Our management team has direct industry knowledge and research experience. We employ local people and contribute to the development of the area.

We have built loyalty with customers at the expense of short-term gain, and aim to be the preferred logistics choice in our target sectors.

With oil discovered in commercial quantities off the coast of Ghana, we are growing into a major player. Our young, energetic leadership supplies quality personal professionalism, technical excellence and inventive thinking to every project we handle.
