About Eddnark

A service provider set up in August 2017 to meet our clients' needs with quality solutions.

Based in the heart of Takoradi

Eddnark Company Limited was registered and incorporated in August 2017 as a service provider. We can supply and deliver to specification, drawing on the experience we have built over the years. We are in Ghana's Western Region.

Our management team has direct knowledge of the industry, extensive research experience and unique administrative skills. We employ local people and contribute to the development of the area.

With oil discovered in commercial quantities off the coast of Ghana, and the potential for more, we are positioned to become a major role player. Our ability to provide quality personal protective equipment (PPE), general logistics support and rental services puts us in a strong place.

We have built loyalty with customers at the expense of short-term gain, and will make sure we become the preferred logistics choice for companies in our target sectors.

Two problems we set out to fix

Reliability and quality

Many logistics and equipment rental vendors in Ghana fall short on reliability, quality and customer satisfaction. We build our service around all three.

Too many vendors for one project

Few vendors offer a wide inventory, so clients have to source equipment from several parties. We hold a wide range, so you deal with one.

Our vision

To be a reliable service provider of equipment rental, logistics services, and engineering and consultancy services in the oil and gas industry, mining sector and construction industry in Ghana.

Our mission

To be seen as the premier provider of equipment and material supply services, manpower supply, general procurement services, and engineering and consultancy services in the country.
