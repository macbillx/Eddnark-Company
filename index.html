About Eddnark

One vendor instead
of many.

Eddnark Company Limited was registered in August 2017 as a service provider, set up to deliver quality solutions to specification. We are based in Takoradi, in Ghana's Western Region.

Clients can lose time coordinating several vendors. Our broad service offering is designed to simplify sourcing and provide dependable support throughout the job.

Our management team brings industry knowledge and research experience. We employ local people and contribute to the development of the area.
