Fabric maintenance

Blasting, coating and maintenance works tailored to each client, delivered with precision and durability.

Protecting structures and equipment

Fabric maintenance keeps steelwork, structures and equipment protected against corrosion and wear. We deliver professional blasting and coating, and reliable, efficient maintenance solutions shaped around the needs of each client.

Our people cover the chain from surveying the problem to inspecting the finished coat, and we also train others as industrial coating instructors.

We deliver engineering and technical expertise, driven by professionalism and innovation.

What it covers

Corrosion mapping

Finding and recording where corrosion is, so work starts in the right place.

Surface preparation

Preparing surfaces properly, led by surface preparation experts.

Blasting

Professional blasting delivered with precision.

Coating application

Coating applied for durability.

Coating inspection

Coating inspectors check the work.

Rope access

Rope access level 1 capability for work at height.

Equipment repairs

Repairs to blasting and coating equipment.

Maintenance works

Maintenance solutions tailored to each client.

Project supervision

Team managers and supervisors keep the work on track.

Backed by our own fleet

Maintenance jobs need power, compressed air, breathing air and light. We rent all of these, including diesel generators, air compressors, a breathing compressor and tower lights, so the equipment and the crew come from one provider.
