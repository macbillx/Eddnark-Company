Procurement and supply chain

Strategic procurement that ensures the timely sourcing and delivery of high-quality materials and equipment.

One team from sourcing to site

Projects slow down when materials, equipment and people come from many different vendors. Our procurement service puts sourcing, purchasing and delivery in the hands of one team that already knows your site and your industry.

Procurement and logistics sit at the centre of how we work. Our team includes a dedicated procurement and logistics lead, and purchasing agents are one of our listed areas of expertise.
