About us

Built around reliable service.

Eddnark Company Limited is a Takoradi-based service provider delivering equipment rental, manpower, procurement and engineering support for critical projects in Ghana.

A dependable partner for industrial operations, logistics and project delivery.

Eddnark Company Limited was registered and incorporated in August 2017 as a service provider. We can supply and deliver to specification, drawing on the experience we have built over the years. We are in Ghana's Western Region and work with project teams that need reliable support on the ground.

Our management team has direct knowledge of the industry, extensive research experience and unique administrative skills. We employ local people and contribute to the development of the area.

With oil discovered in commercial quantities off the coast of Ghana, and the potential for more, we are positioned to become a major role player. Our ability to provide quality personal protective equipment (PPE), general logistics support and rental services puts us in a strong position.

“We have built loyalty with customers at the expense of short-term gain, and will make sure we become the preferred logistics choice for companies in our target sectors.”

We focus on practical delivery, responsive communication and dependable service that helps clients keep work moving.
